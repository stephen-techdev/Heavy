Why 240 GSM

Light cotton feels cheap because it is.

Most tees are printed on 140–180 GSM. It feels thin, goes translucent after a month, and the ribbing twists in the wash. We build the opposite: 240 GSM combed cotton, side-seamed, with a twin-needle hem and shoulder-to-shoulder taping so the tee keeps its shape instead of clinging to it.

  • 240 GSM combed cotton — longer fibres, far less pilling
  • Water-based inks cured at 160°C, so prints never crack
  • Side-seamed and twin-needle stitched throughout
  • Shoulder-to-shoulder taping holds the shape
  • Pre-shrunk — no surprise shrinkage after two washes
